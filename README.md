Zomato Sales Analysis (Power BI)

Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Zomato’s sales performance and customer behavior across multiple Indian cities. The dashboard consolidates key business metrics and demographic insights to support informed, data-driven decision making.

Objectives
Evaluate city-wise sales value, order volume, and customer ratings
Analyze user demographics including age, gender, occupation, and marital status
Identify food preference patterns and year-over-year sales trends

Key Insights & Analysis
Comparative analysis of top-performing cities based on revenue and order count
Visualization of customer segmentation to understand user behavior and preferences
Trend analysis to track business growth and seasonal patterns over time
